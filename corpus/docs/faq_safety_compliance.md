# FAQ: Safety and Compliance

**Audience:** All internal users
**Revision:** 2025-01

## Q: What safety standards do Meridian dock levelers meet?
**A:** All Meridian dock levelers comply with **ANSI MH30.1** (integrated toe guards, hydraulic velocity fuse or equivalent safety device, lip-support systems, maintenance strut). They are compatible with **OSHA 1910.178** requirements for powered industrial truck docking operations.

## Q: Are Meridian industrial doors UL listed?
**A:** Yes. The **RapidRoll 400** and **ThermaGuard 600** operators are UL 325 listed, which covers auto-reverse on obstruction and photo-eye safety requirements for commercial and industrial overhead door operators.

## Q: Do Meridian vehicle restraints meet trailer securement regulations?
**A:** Yes. DockGuard vehicle restraints are engineered for ICC-compliant trailer securement consistent with **49 CFR 392.20**. Note that regulatory compliance also depends on site procedures, driver communication, and operator training — the equipment alone does not guarantee compliance.

## Q: What's the difference between ANSI MH30.1 and OSHA requirements?
**A:** ANSI MH30.1 is the voluntary industry standard for dock leveler design and manufacture. OSHA 1910.178 governs how the equipment must be used and inspected in the workplace. Meridian equipment is designed to ANSI MH30.1 so that operators can meet OSHA requirements with proper procedures.

## Q: Are there seismic zone ratings?
**A:** Seismic ratings vary by product and installation method. Dock levelers installed in a standard pit assembly are not typically subject to seismic bracing requirements because the pit itself is part of the building structure. Free-standing equipment (like some restraint systems) may require site-specific anchorage engineering. Consult a licensed structural engineer for applications in seismic zones 3–4.

## Q: What about wind load?
**A:** Wind load applies primarily to industrial doors. See individual door spec sheets for DASMA 108 wind load class. The RapidRoll 400 is rated Class 2. ThermaGuard 600 standard sizes are rated for 90 mph design wind speed.

## Q: Are annual inspections required?
**A:** Yes, for warranty coverage. See the warranty FAQ and the annual PM checklist. Insurance carriers and OSHA inspection protocols also typically expect documented annual dock equipment inspection.
