# Meridian MD-9000 Air-Powered Dock Leveler — Product Specification

**Product family:** Dock Leveler — Pneumatic
**Model:** MD-9000
**Revision:** 2025-01 (current)

## Overview
The MD-9000 is an air bag–actuated dock leveler with no hydraulic fluid and no electrical components in the leveler itself. Designed for food-grade, freezer, pharmaceutical, and explosion-risk environments where hydraulic contamination is unacceptable.

## Performance
- **Rated lifting capacity:** 40,000 lbs
- **Service range:** ±12 inches from dock height
- **Designed cycle rate:** up to 200 cycles per day
- **Below-dock end load (static):** 12,000 lbs

## Platform sizes (standard)
- 6 ft × 8 ft
- 7 ft × 10 ft (most common)

## Air supply requirements
- 80–100 psi compressed air, 3/4" supply line
- 4 cfm average consumption (single actuation)
- Customer-supplied FRL (filter-regulator-lubricator) recommended

## Actuation
- Dual-chamber reinforced-rubber air bag
- Bag rated 1 million cycles minimum
- Deflate-on-fail design — leveler returns to stored position automatically on air loss

## Environment
- Temperature: -40°F to 120°F
- Suitable for USDA / FDA food-handling zones
- No hydraulic fluid, no oil migration risk
- ATEX-compatible variant available on request (not standard)

## Compliance
- ANSI MH30.1
- OSHA 1910.178 compatible
- USDA acceptable for food-facility dock applications (zone 3)
