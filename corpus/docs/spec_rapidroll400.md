# RapidRoll 400 High-Speed Industrial Door — Product Specification

**Product family:** Industrial Door — High-Speed Roll-Up
**Model:** RapidRoll 400
**Revision:** 2024-11 (current)

## Overview
High-cycle PVC curtain door for interior and exterior openings where speed, traffic flow, and climate separation matter. Soft-edge bottom bar and photo-eye safety curtain.

## Performance
- **Opening speed:** 80 in/sec
- **Closing speed:** 24 in/sec (speed-limited for safety)
- **Cycles per day (rated):** up to 1,500
- **Wind load:** Class 2 per DASMA 108

## Opening sizes
- Width: up to 14 ft
- Height: up to 16 ft

## Curtain
- Material: reinforced PVC, 0.08" nominal thickness
- Colors: blue, orange, yellow, clear-window panel optional
- Operating temperature: 40°F to 140°F
- Tear-away design — curtain reinserts without service call after impact

## Motor and drive
- 1.5 HP inverter-duty motor
- 208V, 230V, or 460V three-phase
- Variable frequency drive integrated
- Encoder-based position control

## Controls
- Control panel: NEMA 4 rated enclosure
- Activation: pull cord, push button, loop detector, motion sensor, or radio (specify at order)
- Relay outputs for interlocks and alarms

## Safety
- Through-beam photo-eye at 6" above floor (auto-reverse)
- Light curtain full-height (optional, recommended for forklift traffic)
- Soft-edge bottom bar with pressure-sensitive reverse
- UL 325 listed

## Compliance
- UL 325
- NEC compliant wiring
- DASMA 108 wind load tested
