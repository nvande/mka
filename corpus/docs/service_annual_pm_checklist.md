# Annual Preventive Maintenance Checklist — Meridian Dock Equipment

**Classification:** Technician only
**Revision:** 2025-01
**Frequency:** Annually, or at 2,000 operating hours, whichever comes first

## Purpose
Annual PM is required to maintain warranty coverage across Meridian dock equipment. Incomplete or undocumented PM voids warranty on affected items.

---

## Dock levelers (all models)

- [ ] Inspect all hinge points for wear and side-play
- [ ] Lubricate per callout chart (lithium grease NLGI #2, or equivalent)
- [ ] Verify safety lip engagement and self-storing action
- [ ] Test velocity fuse / safety leg engagement (cycle at float-down)
- [ ] Inspect toe guards for deformation
- [ ] Check deck plate welds for cracks — focus on lip-to-platform hinge line

### Hydraulic models (MD-7000)
- [ ] Check hydraulic fluid level and color (amber and clear = good; cloudy or dark = replace)
- [ ] Change hydraulic fluid at 2,000 operating hours regardless of condition
- [ ] Verify relief valve pressure setpoint against spec (1,800 psi for 2024+, 1,600 psi legacy)
- [ ] Inspect hoses for abrasion, weeping, and bulges

### Air-powered models (MD-9000)
- [ ] Inspect air bag for cracks, bulges, or delamination
- [ ] Check air supply regulator setting (90 psi nominal)
- [ ] Drain FRL bowl of accumulated condensate

---

## Industrial doors

- [ ] Inspect lift cables for fraying, kinks, or visible wear
- [ ] Check spring balance — door should stay within ±6" of mid-position when released
- [ ] Clean and align photo-eyes (see photo-eye calibration procedure)
- [ ] Test auto-reverse function at three heights
- [ ] Inspect bottom seal / astragal for damage
- [ ] Lubricate rollers, hinges, and bearing plates

### RapidRoll 400 specific
- [ ] Inspect PVC curtain for tears or permanent deformation
- [ ] Verify soft-edge bottom bar pressure reverse
- [ ] Test VFD fault log — clear any recurring events

### ThermaGuard 600 specific
- [ ] Inspect thermal-break joints for damage
- [ ] Verify weather seal compression on closure
- [ ] Check section joint alignment

---

## Vehicle restraints (DockGuard)

- [ ] Verify hook engagement under test load
- [ ] Test all fault codes via diagnostic menu
- [ ] Clean sensor array beneath dock plate
- [ ] Test light tree communication (inside and outside indicators)
- [ ] Verify E-stop reset function

---

## Electrical (all equipment)

- [ ] Torque-check all line-side connections at disconnect
- [ ] Verify GFCI operation where applicable
- [ ] Inspect disconnect operation — clean break, no arcing
- [ ] Check enclosure for moisture ingress

---

## Documentation

All items above must be documented in the equipment maintenance log with date, technician name, and findings. Deficiencies requiring follow-up must be ticketed within 48 hours.
