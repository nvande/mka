# Operator Quick-Start Guide — Dock Equipment

**Audience:** All internal users (intended for end-user operators; safe to share)
**Revision:** 2024-10

## Daily pre-use checks

Before the first use of the shift, any operator should:

1. Visually inspect the dock leveler platform for debris, cracks, or lip damage
2. Verify the dock bumpers are intact and not torn from the wall
3. Confirm the photo-eyes on any powered doors are clean and unobstructed
4. Walk the approach area for ice, spills, or obstructions

Do not operate equipment showing visible damage. Tag it out and notify the supervisor.

## Using a hydraulic dock leveler (MD-7000)

1. Ensure the trailer is fully engaged against the dock bumpers
2. Engage the vehicle restraint (DockGuard) — wait for green light at dock
3. Press and hold the RAISE button until the leveler lifts and the lip extends
4. Release the button — leveler will lower onto the trailer bed
5. Verify the lip rests on the trailer floor before driving equipment onto it

## Using a mechanical dock leveler (MD-5000)

1. Pull the activation chain firmly to release the hold-down
2. Walk the platform up — it will rise under spring force
3. Walk forward until the lip extends
4. Step back; platform will lower onto the trailer with the lip engaged

## Safe return to stored position

- Clear the platform of personnel and equipment before lowering
- For MD-7000: press the LOWER button; the leveler returns automatically
- For MD-5000: walk onto the platform gently; it will lower under weight

## What NOT to do

- **Never** stand on the lip while the platform is raised
- **Never** operate the leveler with personnel between the trailer and the dock
- **Never** exceed the posted capacity label at the dock
- **Never** attempt repairs — report faults to supervisor or service
- **Never** disable or bypass safety sensors, photo-eyes, or restraints

## Reporting a problem

Note the equipment ID (stenciled or placarded at each dock), describe the symptom, note the time and date, and report to the supervisor. If the equipment is unsafe, lock it out and mark the dock as out-of-service.
