# Meridian MD-7000 Hydraulic Dock Leveler — Product Specification

**Product family:** Dock Leveler — Hydraulic
**Model:** MD-7000
**Revision:** 2025-01 (current — supersedes 2021-03 revision)

## Overview
The MD-7000 is a push-button hydraulic dock leveler for standard to heavy-duty operations. Best-selling model in the Meridian line. Three-phase electrical required.

## Performance
- **Rated lifting capacity:** 35,000 lbs (revised 2024 — earlier revisions rated at 30,000 lbs; see Engineering Bulletin EB-2024-003)
- **Service range:** ±15 inches from dock height
- **Designed cycle rate:** up to 150 cycles per day
- **Below-dock end load (static):** 10,000 lbs

## Platform sizes (standard)
- 6 ft × 8 ft
- 7 ft × 8 ft
- 7 ft × 10 ft

## Power requirements
- **Motor:** 2 HP TEFC
- **Voltage options:** 208V, 230V, or 460V, three-phase, 60 Hz
- **Circuit:** 30-amp dedicated, fused disconnect within 20 ft of leveler

## Hydraulic system
- Integrated power unit, factory-filled
- Fluid: ISO VG 32 hydraulic oil (AW-32 equivalent)
- Relief valve factory-set to 1,800 psi
- Velocity fuse engages automatically on hydraulic line failure

## Safety features
- Full-width integrated toe guards per ANSI MH30.1
- Automatic velocity fuse (hydraulic)
- Maintenance strut with lockout provision
- Night lock / cross-traffic float position

## Operating environment
- Temperature: -20°F to 120°F

## Compliance
- ANSI MH30.1
- OSHA 1910.178 compatible
- AWS D1.1 welded construction
