# ThermaGuard 600 Insulated Sectional Door — Product Specification

**Product family:** Industrial Door — Insulated Sectional
**Model:** ThermaGuard 600
**Revision:** 2024-09 (current)

## Overview
Insulated sectional overhead door for climate-controlled warehouses, cold storage, and energy-efficient facilities. CFC-free polyurethane core, thermal-break frame construction.

## Thermal performance
- **R-value:** 17.5 (panel)
- **U-factor:** 0.057
- **Air infiltration:** 0.3 cfm/ft² at 25 mph
- Suitable for cooler and freezer applications with heated track option

## Construction
- Panel skin: 26-gauge steel both sides
- Panel thickness: 2"
- Panel core: CFC-free polyurethane, density 2.2 lb/ft³
- Thermal-break aluminum section joints
- Weather seals: full perimeter, between-section, header, and bottom astragal

## Opening sizes
- Width: up to 20 ft standard
- Height: up to 20 ft standard
- Custom sizes available with 8–10 week lead time

## Lift types
- Standard lift
- High lift
- Full vertical lift
- Follow-the-roof

## Operator
- Standard: LiftMaster J commercial jackshaft, 1/2 HP
- Optional: 3/4 HP for high-cycle applications
- Integrated safety: photo-eye, sensing edge, manual disconnect

## Wind load
- Rated for 90 mph design wind speed (standard sizes)
- Higher ratings available on specified models

## Compliance
- UL 325 (operators)
- DASMA 108 wind load standards
- ENERGY STAR partner program
