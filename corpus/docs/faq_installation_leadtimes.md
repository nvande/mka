# FAQ: Installation Lead Times

**Audience:** All internal users
**Revision:** 2025-02 (updated quarterly)

## Standard lead times (order to ship)

| Product | Standard lead time |
|---|---|
| MD-5000 mechanical dock leveler | 4–6 weeks |
| MD-7000 hydraulic dock leveler | 4–6 weeks |
| MD-9000 air-powered dock leveler | 6–8 weeks |
| RapidRoll 400 high-speed door (standard sizes) | 5–7 weeks |
| ThermaGuard 600 insulated door (standard sizes) | 4–5 weeks |
| ThermaGuard 600 custom sizes | 8–10 weeks |
| DockGuard vehicle restraint | 5–7 weeks |

## On-site installation time

- Dock leveler: 1–2 days per unit (assumes pit is prepped)
- Industrial door: 1 day per opening (assumes opening is framed)
- Vehicle restraint: 1 day per bay (plus electrical/air rough-in)

## Customer responsibilities
- Pit construction and drainage (dock levelers)
- Opening framing and structural backing (doors)
- Electrical service to within 20 ft of equipment location
- Compressed air supply for MD-9000 and DockGuard

## Expedite options
Expedited shipping is available with a 20% surcharge on eligible products. Custom ThermaGuard sizes cannot be expedited below 6 weeks due to panel manufacturing constraints.

## Seasonal notes
Lead times typically extend 1–2 weeks during Q4 (October–December) due to peak demand. Plan accordingly for year-end facility projects.
