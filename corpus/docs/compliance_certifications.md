## Company-level certifications

Meridian operates under an **ISO 9001:2015** certified quality management system. Certification is audited annually. Certificate available on request for customer bid packages.

## Regional market notes

Meridian equipment is sold primarily in the United States and Canada. Products are designed to North American standards (ANSI, UL, NEC, OSHA) and are not currently offered with European or international market certifications as stock configurations. Customers requiring international market certifications should contact engineering for project-specific review.

## Documentation available on request

- Certificates of compliance per product family
- ISO 9001 certificate
- Test reports (DASMA wind load, UL 325 operator testing)
- Material certifications (steel mill certs) for project bid packages

## Notes for field sales

Do not represent equipment as meeting standards not listed in this document. If a customer requests a standard we don't explicitly meet, route the inquiry to engineering rather than making assumptions.
