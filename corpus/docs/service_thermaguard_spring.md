# Service Procedure: ThermaGuard 600 Spring Tension Adjustment

**Equipment:** ThermaGuard 600 Insulated Sectional Door
**Classification:** Technician only — certified door technicians only
**Revision:** 2024-06

> ⚠ **DANGER — TORSION SPRING HAZARD**
> Torsion springs under tension store substantial energy. Improper adjustment or slippage can cause severe injury or death. This procedure must be performed by two qualified technicians using manufacturer-supplied winding bars. Do not improvise tooling.

## Prerequisites
- Two qualified technicians present throughout procedure
- Manufacturer winding bars (part **TG-WB-PAIR**)
- Two C-clamps (6" minimum)
- Safety glasses and gloves (required)

## Procedure

### Step 1 — Close and secure
Fully close the door. Disengage the operator by pulling the emergency release cord. Verify door is in fully closed position.

### Step 2 — Clamp door
Install C-clamps on the vertical track just below the bottom roller on each side. This prevents upward travel if springs release.

### Step 3 — Identify tension direction
For left-wound springs (most installations): winding increases tension by turning the bar upward on the right-hand side. Invert for right-wound springs. Verify by inspecting the coil wind direction before proceeding.

### Step 4 — Wind or unwind
Insert the first winding bar into the winding cone. Apply force in the required direction — one bar at a time, alternating — until the target turn count is reached.

**Target tension:** one full turn per foot of door height, **plus 1/4 turn**.

Example: For a 12-ft tall door, target is 12.25 turns.

### Step 5 — Secure winding cone
Tighten the winding cone set screws to manufacturer spec (10 ft-lb). Remove winding bars only after both set screws are fully tightened.

### Step 6 — Test balance
Remove C-clamps. With operator still disengaged, manually lift the door to mid-travel and release. A properly balanced door stays within ±6" of mid-position. If it drifts significantly, repeat adjustment in 1/4-turn increments.

### Step 7 — Reconnect operator
Re-engage the operator. Run a full open/close cycle and observe for smooth motion.

## Critical rules
- **Never** adjust springs with the door open.
- **Never** use non-manufacturer winding bars — rebar and improvised tools slip under load.
- If a spring is broken, replace **both** springs as a pair, even if only one is visibly failed.
