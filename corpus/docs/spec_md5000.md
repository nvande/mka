# Meridian MD-5000 Mechanical Dock Leveler — Product Specification

**Product family:** Dock Leveler — Mechanical
**Model:** MD-5000
**Revision:** 2025-02 (current)

## Overview
The MD-5000 is a spring-counterbalanced, lip-activated mechanical dock leveler designed for light to medium-duty dock operations where electrical or hydraulic power is unavailable or undesired. Activation is manual via pull chain.

## Performance
- **Rated lifting capacity:** 30,000 lbs
- **Service range:** ±12 inches from dock height
- **Designed cycle rate:** up to 50 cycles per day
- **Below-dock end load (static):** 7,500 lbs

## Platform sizes (standard)
- 6 ft × 6 ft
- 6 ft × 8 ft
- 7 ft × 8 ft

## Construction
- Platform: A36 structural steel, 1/4" checker plate deck
- Lip: 16" hinged, 5/8" thick, self-storing
- Hinge: greaseable, full-width piano hinge
- Finish: gray enamel over rust-inhibitive primer

## Safety features
- Automatic safety leg engagement on float-down
- Velocity safety — limits descent rate if lip loses support
- Full-width toe guards per ANSI MH30.1
- Integrated maintenance strut (yellow, high-visibility)

## Operating environment
- Temperature: -20°F to 120°F
- No electrical, hydraulic, or air supply required

## Compliance
- ANSI MH30.1
- OSHA 1910.178 compatible

## Typical applications
Retail distribution cross-docks, light manufacturing, facilities without three-phase power at the dock face.
