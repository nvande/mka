# Meridian MD-7000 Hydraulic Dock Leveler — Product Specification

**Product family:** Dock Leveler — Hydraulic
**Model:** MD-7000
**Revision:** 2021-03 (SUPERSEDED — retained for legacy reference only)

> **Note:** This revision has been superseded by the 2025-01 revision. Engineering Bulletin EB-2024-003 documents the capacity and motor upgrades introduced in 2024. This document is retained for service reference on units manufactured before serial number 7K-2024-00001.

## Overview
The MD-7000 is a push-button hydraulic dock leveler for standard dock operations. Three-phase electrical required.

## Performance
- **Rated lifting capacity:** 30,000 lbs
- **Service range:** ±12 inches from dock height
- **Designed cycle rate:** up to 120 cycles per day
- **Below-dock end load (static):** 8,000 lbs

## Platform sizes
- 6 ft × 8 ft
- 7 ft × 8 ft

## Power requirements
- **Motor:** 1.5 HP TEFC
- **Voltage:** 208V, 230V, or 460V, three-phase, 60 Hz

## Hydraulic system
- Integrated power unit
- Fluid: ISO VG 32 hydraulic oil
- Relief valve factory-set to 1,600 psi

## Safety features
- Full-width toe guards per ANSI MH30.1
- Velocity fuse
- Maintenance strut

## Compliance
- ANSI MH30.1
- OSHA 1910.178 compatible
