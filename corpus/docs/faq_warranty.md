# FAQ: Warranty Coverage

**Audience:** All internal users
**Revision:** 2025-01

## Dock levelers

| Component | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Platform and frame (structural) | 5 years |
| Hydraulic components (MD-7000) | 2 years |
| Air bag and pneumatic components (MD-9000) | 2 years |
| Electrical components (motor, controls) | 1 year |
| Paint finish | 1 year |

## Industrial doors

| Component | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Panels (ThermaGuard 600) | 5 years |
| PVC curtain (RapidRoll 400) | 3 years |
| Springs and cables | 2 years |
| Operators and controls | 1 year |
| Photo-eyes and safety sensors | 1 year |

## Vehicle restraints
Comprehensive 2-year warranty on all components except wear items (sensor plate cover, hook polymer wear strip).

## What voids warranty
- Operation outside rated capacity
- Operation outside specified environmental limits
- Unauthorized modifications or repairs
- Failure to perform documented annual PM
- Operation at hydraulic pressures exceeding spec (MD-7000: 2,100 psi absolute limit)
- Use of non-specified fluids (hydraulic oil other than ISO VG 32 / AW-32)
- Impact damage

## What's covered
Manufacturing defects, premature failure of components under normal use within specified cycle rates, and failures traceable to design or material defects.

## Claim process
1. Document the failure: photos, service notes, error codes if applicable
2. Submit warranty claim through distributor portal with equipment serial number
3. Meridian reviews within 5 business days
4. Approved claims ship replacement parts ground freight prepaid; labor is not covered under standard warranty (see service contract pricing for labor coverage options)
