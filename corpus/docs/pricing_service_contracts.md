# Service Contract Pricing — 2026

**Audience:** Sales team only
**Revision:** 2026-01

## Tier comparison

### Silver — $680 per unit per year
- Annual preventive maintenance visit (per PM checklist)
- Documentation provided to customer for warranty compliance
- Call-out labor billed at $125/hr portal-to-portal
- Parts at list price

### Gold — $1,240 per unit per year
- Everything in Silver
- Call-out labor at discounted $95/hr portal-to-portal
- Priority scheduling (48-hour response target within primary service area)
- 10% parts discount

### Platinum — $2,100 per unit per year
- Everything in Gold
- Call-out labor included (covered) on all warranty-eligible repairs
- Consumables (hydraulic fluid, filters, lubricants) included
- 4-hour response SLA within primary service area
- Annual performance report with uptime metrics

## Multi-site discounts
- 5–9 sites under contract: 10% off contract price
- 10–24 sites: 15% off
- 25+ sites: 20% off and dedicated account manager

## Exclusions
- Impact damage, vandalism, natural disaster
- Equipment operated outside rated capacity or environment
- Modifications or repairs not performed by authorized technicians
- Hydraulic fluid changes beyond annual (billed separately)

## SLA notes
Response SLAs are only guaranteed within 50 miles of an authorized service center. Beyond 50 miles, response times are best-effort and mileage/travel time apply.

## Renewal
Contracts auto-renew annually with 60-day written notice to cancel. Annual price escalation capped at lesser of CPI or 5%.
