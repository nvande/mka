# Service Procedure: MD-7000 Hydraulic Pressure Reset

**Equipment:** Meridian MD-7000 Hydraulic Dock Leveler (all revisions)
**Classification:** Technician only — do not distribute to operators
**Revision:** 2025-01

## Symptoms indicating pressure reset may be required
- Leveler fails to lower under load
- Chattering or pulsing during descent
- Audible relief valve chatter at rest
- Lip will not extend at factory rest pressure

## Required tools
- 9/16" open-end wrench
- Hydraulic pressure gauge 0–3,000 psi (test-port compatible)
- Torque wrench 10–50 ft-lb
- Lockout/tagout kit

## Procedure

### Step 1 — Isolate power
Engage the dock leveler disconnect to the OFF position and apply lockout/tagout per facility procedure. Verify zero voltage at the leveler junction box.

### Step 2 — Locate the relief valve
The relief valve is on the integrated hydraulic power unit, identifiable by the brass adjustment screw with a 9/16" locknut. The 1/8" NPT test port is directly below the valve body.

### Step 3 — Install pressure gauge
Remove the test-port plug and install the pressure gauge. Apply Teflon tape to threads.

### Step 4 — Adjust relief valve
Loosen the locknut with the 9/16" wrench. Turn the adjustment screw:
- **Clockwise** to increase pressure
- **Counterclockwise** to decrease pressure

Factory preset is 1,800 psi (units manufactured 2024-present). Legacy units manufactured before 2024 are preset to 1,600 psi.

### Step 5 — Verify
Restore power. Run three complete lift-and-lower cycles under no load. Observe gauge during descent — pressure should hold within ±100 psi of setpoint.

### Step 6 — Tighten locknut
Torque locknut to 15 ft-lb. Remove gauge and reinstall test-port plug.

## Safety limits
- **Never** exceed 2,100 psi. Operation above this limit risks rupture of hydraulic hoses and voids warranty.
- If pressure remains erratic after adjustment, replace the relief valve cartridge (part number **MD7-HV-42**).

## Documentation
Log date, technician name, and resulting pressure in the equipment maintenance log.
