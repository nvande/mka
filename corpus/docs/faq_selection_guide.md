# FAQ: Which Dock Leveler Should I Choose?

**Audience:** All internal users
**Revision:** 2025-02

## Q: We have no three-phase power at the dock. What do you recommend?
**A:** The **MD-5000** mechanical dock leveler requires no electrical or hydraulic power — it's spring-activated and manually operated via pull chain. Best for docks with fewer than 50 cycles per day. For higher cycle rates, retrofitting three-phase service is usually more economical than using multiple mechanical units.

## Q: What's the best all-purpose dock leveler?
**A:** For the majority of distribution and manufacturing applications, the **MD-7000** hydraulic leveler is the default recommendation. It handles up to 150 cycles per day, has a 35,000 lb capacity, and operates on standard three-phase power.

## Q: We're a cold storage or food facility. Which leveler?
**A:** The **MD-9000** air-powered leveler is the right choice. It has no hydraulic fluid, eliminating oil contamination risk in food-handling environments. It also operates cleanly down to -40°F. Pair it with a **ThermaGuard 600** insulated sectional door for thermal separation, and consider a **RapidRoll 400** interior door for high-traffic freezer thresholds.

## Q: We have heavy forklift traffic — wheel loads over 30,000 lbs. What capacity do we need?
**A:** Look at below-dock end load ratings, not just lifting capacity. The MD-7000 supports 10,000 lbs static below-dock end load; the MD-9000 supports 12,000 lbs. For heaviest applications, the MD-9000 is preferred.

## Q: Can I use a mechanical leveler in a cold storage freezer?
**A:** The MD-5000 has an operating temperature floor of -20°F. It will function in a dock vestibule or refrigerated zone but is not rated for true freezer environments (-20°F and below). For those, specify the MD-9000 air-powered model.

## Q: What platform size should I order?
**A:** Rule of thumb: platform width should match or exceed the widest trailer opening you service (typically 7 ft). Platform length depends on the range of trailer bed heights — longer platforms give more working range. 7×10 is the most versatile size for mixed fleets.

## Q: What about explosion-risk or hazardous locations?
**A:** Meridian does not sell a stock explosion-proof dock leveler. The MD-9000's pneumatic actuation has no electrical components in the leveler itself, making it the closest standard product for use near vapor-prone areas — but the control station is still electrical. Contact engineering for ATEX or Class I Div 1/2 applications.
