# Service Procedure: RapidRoll 400 Photo-Eye Calibration

**Equipment:** RapidRoll 400 High-Speed Industrial Door
**Classification:** Technician only
**Revision:** 2024-11
**Frequency:** Annually, or after any control panel or photo-eye replacement

## Required tools
- Lens cleaning cloth (microfiber)
- Isopropyl alcohol (70%)
- Multimeter
- Small flat-blade screwdriver

## Procedure

### Step 1 — Clean lenses
Clean both sender and receiver lenses with microfiber cloth and isopropyl alcohol. Inspect for cracks or impact damage. Replace the pair if either lens is compromised.

### Step 2 — Verify supply voltage
At terminal **TB4-7** in the control panel, verify 24VDC ±10%. If voltage is absent or out of tolerance, check the 24VDC supply module before proceeding.

### Step 3 — Check alignment LED
Observe the receiver unit's alignment LED:
- **Steady green** — alignment is correct, proceed to Step 5
- **Blinking green** — sender is misaligned, continue to Step 4
- **Steady red** — no signal detected; replace photo-eye pair (part **RR4-PE-SET**)

### Step 4 — Align sender
Loosen the sender mounting bracket set screw. Slowly pivot the sender until the receiver's alignment LED changes from blinking to steady green. Tighten set screw without disturbing alignment. Reverify steady green.

### Step 5 — Break-beam test
Close the door to approximately mid-height. Interrupt the photo-eye beam by hand. Verify door reverses within **0.5 seconds**. Repeat test at three different heights (low, mid, high).

### Step 6 — Documentation
Log calibration in maintenance record with date, technician initials, and test results.

## Related parts
- Photo-eye pair (sender + receiver): **RR4-PE-SET**
- Alignment bracket kit: **RR4-PE-BRK**
